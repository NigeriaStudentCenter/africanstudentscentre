Student training programme

The Completion-Readiness Programme

A structured course for the African students you admit. It runs from offer to the end of the first term, and every module is tied to a concern that affects your BCA results: visa refusals, students who never enrol, and students who withdraw.

Curriculum

Three stages, eight modules, six live sessions

Lessons are short, mobile-first and designed to work on low data. Live sessions are scheduled in West, East and Southern African time zones and recorded.

Stage 1Visa readiness

3 hoursWithin 10 days of offerProtects refusal rate

Completed before the CAS is issued or the visa application goes in, so it never holds up your admissions timeline.

Module 1Your course, your plan

60 min

Online lessons and a written exercise

  • Explaining why this course, this university and the UK
  • Linking the course to past study, work and career plans
  • Explaining gaps in study or employment honestly
  • What a credibility interview is, and how it is assessed

Evidence: a written study-plan statement, reviewed by our team

Module 2Money and documents that meet the rules

60 min

Online lessons and a checklist

  • Maintenance requirements and how long funds must be held
  • Acceptable bank statements, sponsor letters and loan letters
  • Why borrowed or "shown" funds lead to refusals
  • A fee plan for the whole course, not just year one

Evidence: a completed funding checklist and full-course fee plan

Module 3Honest applications

30 min

Online lesson and case studies

  • What counts as deception, and the long-term bans it can bring
  • Risks of agents who alter or "improve" documents
  • The student's own responsibility for everything submitted

Evidence: a signed honesty declaration

SessionOne-to-one mock credibility interview

30 min

Live video call with a trained interviewer

  • A realistic interview based on the student's own application
  • Structured feedback and a second attempt if needed
  • Concerns flagged to your admissions team before the CAS

Evidence: an interview rating (ready, ready with support, or not ready) shared with you

Stage 2Pre-departure

6 hours2–4 weeks before travelProtects enrolment rate

Starts once the visa is granted. It gets students to campus on time and ready to keep their visa conditions.

Module 4Your visa conditions

60 min

Online lessons and a quiz

  • Term-time work limits and what counts as work
  • Attendance and engagement monitoring, and why it matters
  • Reporting changes of address, course or circumstances
  • Using your eVisa and UKVI account
  • What happens if conditions are broken

Evidence: quiz passed at 80% or above

Module 5Arrive and enrol

45 min

Online lessons and a personal arrival plan

  • Your university's enrolment dates and deadlines
  • Travel, first-night accommodation and getting to campus
  • What to do, and who to tell, if you are delayed
  • Documents to carry and the first-week checklist

Evidence: a confirmed arrival plan shared with your international office

Module 6Managing money in the UK

60 min

Online lessons and a budget tool

  • Realistic monthly costs in your university's city
  • Planning for currency swings and transfer delays
  • Fee instalments, and what to do before missing one
  • Common rent, job and visa scams aimed at new students

Evidence: a completed first-year budget

Module 7Studying in the UK

90 min

Online lessons and practice tasks

  • Independent study, seminars and how teaching is organised
  • Essays, reports and exams: what markers look for
  • Referencing and avoiding plagiarism
  • Your university's rules on AI tools
  • Asking for help early: tutors, study skills and extensions

Evidence: a referencing exercise and an academic integrity quiz

Module 8Wellbeing, safety and settling in

45 min

Online lessons

  • Culture shock and the first months away from home
  • Registering with a GP and using the NHS
  • University support services and student communities
  • Staying safe, and who to contact in a crisis

Evidence: module completion

SessionLive pre-departure briefing

60 min

Live group webinar, recorded

  • Questions answered by our team and, if you wish, your staff
  • Advice from current students from the same countries
  • Final check that each student has a confirmed arrival date

Evidence: attendance, or the recording watched

Stage 3First-term support

1 hr 45 min, plus optional 1 hr 30 minWeeks 2–12Protects completion rate

Short check-ins at the points where students usually start to struggle, so problems reach you while they can still be fixed.

Check-in 1 · Week 2Settling in

30 min

One-to-one call

  • Enrolment, accommodation, bank account and GP done?
  • Early worries about money, work or the course

Evidence: check-in record, with any concerns referred to you

Check-in 2 · Week 6First assessments

30 min

One-to-one call

  • Attendance, first coursework and feedback
  • Working hours kept within visa limits

Evidence: check-in record, with any concerns referred to you

Review · Weeks 11–12End-of-term review

45 min

One-to-one call

  • Results so far, and a plan for term two
  • Fee plan for year two reviewed against exchange rates

Evidence: a programme completion certificate

OptionalPeer circles

2 × 45 min

Small online groups, led by a facilitator

  • Students from similar backgrounds sharing what is working
  • An early warning sign when someone stops turning up

Evidence: attendance

  1. Offer

    Stage 1 starts

    Student is enrolled on the programme as soon as you send the offer.

  2. Before CAS

    Interview rating sent

    You receive each student's readiness rating before deciding on the CAS.

  3. Visa granted

    Stage 2 starts

    Pre-departure modules and the live briefing, finished before travel.

  4. Weeks 2–12

    Stage 3 check-ins

    Three check-ins, and a completion certificate at the end of term one.

Reporting

What your team receives

  • Readiness rating before the CASEach student's mock interview result and funding checklist status, so your team has more evidence for the decision.
  • Cohort progress dashboardWhich students have finished each module, and who is falling behind.
  • Early-warning referralsConcerns from check-ins about money, attendance, work hours or wellbeing, sent to a named contact. Shared with the student's consent, in line with UK GDPR.
  • End-of-intake reportCompletion rates by stage, and the refusal and enrolment results for trained students, so you can compare them with the rest of your cohort.
  • Your content includedYour enrolment dates, induction, attendance policy and support contacts are built into Modules 4, 5 and 7.
  • Flexible scopeTake the full programme, or Stage 1 or Stage 2 on their own, for a pilot intake.
