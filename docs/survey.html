Where does admitting African students put the most pressure on your university?

From 1 June 2026, UKVI's Basic Compliance Assessment allows a visa refusal rate of under 5% and expects at least 95% of CAS holders to enrol. The course completion threshold of 85% rises to 90% from 1 June 2027. A small number of avoidable cases can now move a sponsor towards amber or red.

We're asking UK university teams where the pressure really sits, so the support we provide matches it. Most questions are one click. No answer is shared with UKVI or with other universities.
